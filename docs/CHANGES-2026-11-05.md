# Changes 2026-11-05 (steps 1 and 2 of docs/requirements finished)

What was left of the suggested order's first two steps (`docs/requirements/README.md`) is in.
Tests: `tests/test_orchestration.py` (new), plus additions to `test_safety.py`, `test_execution.py`,
`test_contracts.py` and `test_evolution.py`.

## Budgets (S04-R-06, S04-R-09, S14-R-03)

- A goal whose budget runs out is **paused** and administrators are notified (Settings →
  `budget_exhausted_action`: `pause`, default, or `stop`, the old behaviour). An iteration cut short by
  the budget is not judged; its unrun tasks run after the resume. Resume refuses while the goal is still
  over budget.
- Goals can carry a cost estimate (`estimated_cost_usd` on the order, or the planner's). Spending more
  than twice it pauses the goal; resuming accepts the cost so far as the new estimate.
- A retry that would wait past the goal's time budget is not made.

## Recovery (S04-R-05)

- The plan step and every finished writing task are committed on the goal branch. After a restart the
  worktree is reset to the last of these commits, so a task cut off mid-write reruns without its partial
  edits. Each task's changed files now come from its commit.

## Planning and review (S04-R-06, G-R-01, S04-R-03)

- An invalid plan goes back to the planner once, with the validation error, before the standard plan.
- A check that fails in `escalate_after` (2) iterations in a row is escalated to the planner (level 2).
- Task contracts gain `in_scope`, `out_of_scope` (a change there fails the task and counts as a scope
  violation), `risk_level` (high adds a security review, done by QA while that room is disabled),
  `test_plan`, `budget` (`cost_usd`, `turns` → Claude Code `--max-turns`), `deadline` (caps the run's
  timeout), `requirement_keys` and `decision_topic`. `FINDING:` and `NEXT:` lines are kept on the task.
- Tasks that recommend differently on one `decision_topic` get one QA review, whose decision is recorded
  with every task as evidence; financial and critical ones wait for a person.

## Evolution (S13-D-02)

- A new candidate playbook first runs the smoke suite against the current playbook, in the background;
  only then is it tried on users' goals (Settings → `candidate_smoke`). Eval goals no longer steer
  evolution.

## Dashboard

- Goal view: why a paused goal is paused and what resume does, its cost estimate, and each task's
  recommendation, findings and next steps.
